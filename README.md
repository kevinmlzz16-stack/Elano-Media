# Elano-Media

Elano Media Growth & Brand Authority.

## Struktur Paket Layanan Media Sosial

### 🟢 1. Basic Growth Package

**Cocok untuk:** UMKM / bisnis baru.

**Deliverables:**
- 8 konten feed (desain + copywriting)
- 2 reels simple (motion basic / slideshow)
- Caption + hashtag research
- 1 platform (Instagram)
- Content planning bulanan
- Monthly insight report sederhana

**Harga rekomendasi:** Rp 1.500.000 – Rp 2.500.000 / bulan

**Value positioning:** Fokus membangun konsistensi dan branding awal.

---

### 🔵 2. Professional Brand Package

**Cocok untuk:** bisnis berkembang yang ingin scale.

**Deliverables:**
- 12 feed konten premium
- 4 reels engaging (hook strategy)
- Copywriting storytelling + CTA conversion
- 2 platform (Instagram + TikTok)
- Content calendar detail
- Basic competitor research
- Monthly analytics report + evaluasi strategi
- 1x meeting strategi per bulan

**Harga rekomendasi:** Rp 3.500.000 – Rp 6.000.000 / bulan

**Value positioning:** Fokus ke engagement + awareness + mulai arahkan ke konversi.

---

### 🟣 3. Elite Social Domination Package

**Cocok untuk:** brand serius & corporate.

**Deliverables:**
- 16–20 konten feed high quality
- 6–8 reels dengan hook psychology
- Scriptwriting untuk video
- 2–3 platform
- Brand positioning strategy
- Content pillar strategy
- Audience persona mapping
- Ads strategy (belum termasuk budget ads)
- Monthly performance dashboard
- 2x strategy meeting
- Prioritas revisi & fast response

**Harga rekomendasi:** Rp 8.000.000 – Rp 15.000.000 / bulan

**Value positioning:** Fokus dominasi awareness + conversion + growth sistematis.
